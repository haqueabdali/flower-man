# Flower Man — Application kit

Checklist, founder profile template, market validation plan, outreach plan and 90-day roadmap (24 November 2026 – 21 February 2027).

## 1. Application preparation checklist

### Company and identity
- [ ] Digital identity for every founder (SPID, CIE or CNS)
- [ ] Digital signature (*firma digitale*) for the legal representative
- [ ] Company certified email (PEC)
- [ ] Founders' agreement: shares, roles, vesting / good-leaver, IP transfer to the company
- [ ] SRL incorporation deed and articles (*atto costitutivo e statuto*) with investor-ready clauses and a technology-focused business purpose (*oggetto sociale*)
- [ ] Registration in the Registro delle Imprese, then in the *sezione speciale startup innovative* (self-declaration form)
- [ ] Software registration with SIAE (*Registro pubblico speciale per i programmi per elaboratore*) in the company's name
- [ ] Company bank account; Stripe account in the company's name

### Smart&Start Italia
- [ ] Business plan (`01-business-plan.md`, finalised)
- [ ] 24-month investment plan and financial forecast (`04-financial-model.xlsx`)
- [ ] Sources and uses (*fonti e impieghi*), showing the own contribution (angel money / share capital)
- [ ] Quotes (*preventivi*) for planned purchases and services
- [ ] Founder CVs (template below)
- [ ] Innovation description: registered software, R&D work package
- [ ] Pilot results (florists signed, orders, ratings)
- [ ] Application submitted in the Invitalia online area

### Nuova Impresa 2026 (deadline 12:00, 29 January 2027, or earlier if funds run out)
- [ ] Company opened in Lombardy between 1 Jun 2025 and 31 Dec 2026, registered and active
- [ ] Check that your ATECO code is not excluded
- [ ] Eligible spending of at least €3,000 planned and documented (check the eligible list in the call)
- [ ] Application on the Restart (Infocamere) platform

### Investors
- [ ] Pitch deck (`02-pitch-deck.md` → slides)
- [ ] One-page executive summary (`03-project-description-it-en.md`)
- [ ] Product tour PDF and 50-second commercial
- [ ] Simple term sheet for the angel round [with accountant/lawyer]
- [ ] Statement that the company is a registered innovative startup (for the 65% deduction)

## 3. Market validation plan (before applying to Smart&Start)

| Test | How | Success signal |
|---|---|---|
| Florist interest | visit 30 Bergamo florists with the partner app and a one-page offer | ≥ 10 sign up; ≥ 5 upload catalogues |
| Courier supply | recruit 5–8 couriers (bike/scooter) | ≥ 3 active in pilot |
| Customer demand | 6–8 week pilot in central Bergamo; welcome code; florists share QR codes | ≥ 300 orders, rating ≥ 4.5, ≥ 25% repeat within 60 days |
| Basket size | measure actual average flowers subtotal | ≥ €40 |
| Willingness to pay | test commission at 15% vs 18% with florists; test delivery fee | florists accept ≥ 15%; < 10% checkout drop at €5.90 |
| Peak readiness | run Valentine's Day (14 Feb 2027) as a stress test | on-time ≥ 90% |

## 4. Outreach plan

- **Invitalia:** read the current Smart&Start rules and FAQ, then use the contact form in the Invitalia area for questions before applying.
- **Camera di Commercio di Bergamo:** book the start-up desk for incorporation and innovative-startup registration.
- **Incubators:** PoliHub (applications open without a company), Kilometro Rosso / POINT Dalmine (ask about consumer-startup support), Bergamo University entrepreneurship office.
- **Angels:** Italian Angels for Growth (check their submission route), personal network, Bergamo entrepreneurs. Pitch the 65% IRPEF deduction.
- **Accelerators:** watch CDP Venture Capital *Rete Nazionale Acceleratori* calls for marketplace/consumer verticals.

Never imply an organisation has committed until it has in writing.
