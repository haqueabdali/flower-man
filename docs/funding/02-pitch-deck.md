# Flower Man — Investor pitch deck (12 slides, content draft)

Use with the screenshots from the product tour PDF and the 50-second commercial. Figures are estimates from `04-financial-model.xlsx`.

**1 · Title**
Flower Man — *From your heart to their doorstep.*
Your city's florists, delivered today. Bergamo, 2027.
[Founder names · contact]

**2 · Problem**
- Ordering flowers online means national networks and fixed catalogues; your local florist is invisible online.
- Independent florists can't afford e-commerce, couriers or marketing.
- Demand spikes on a few dates (14 Feb, 8 March, Mother's Day) that shops can't staff.

**3 · Solution**
One marketplace, three apps.
- **Customers** choose the florist, pick a delivery slot, track the courier, get a photo at the door.
- **Florists** set their own catalogue and prices.
- **Couriers** pick nearby jobs, with fee and tip shown up front.

**4 · Product (it's built)**
Screens: shop → checkout → tracking → delivered photo → review; partner orders; courier jobs; admin dashboard.
- Customer app, partner app and admin panel complete.
- 150 unit and 72 integration tests; CI green; Italian and English.

**5 · What's unique**
- Custom bouquet requests: florists compete with offers and photos.
- Same-day, tracked, photo-proven delivery.
- Fair split: florists keep 85% at launch, couriers keep 100% of fees and tips.

**6 · Market**
- Italian household flower and plant spend ≈ €1.9B *(verify)*; sector ≈ €3.3B *(verify)*.
- Launch: Bergamo (≈ 1.1M people in the province), then Lombardy.

**7 · Business model**
- Commission on flowers: 15% at launch, then 18–20%.
- Featured placement for florists.
- About €7 contribution per order by year 3 (estimate).

**8 · Go-to-market**
- Supply first: 10–15 Bergamo florists signed before launch.
- Florists promote to their own customers; peak-date campaigns; welcome codes; reminders for repeat orders.

**10 · Competition**
2×2 chart: local choice (low ↔ high) × same-day tracked delivery (low ↔ high).
- Interflora / relay networks: low local choice, medium delivery.
- Farm-direct brands: low local choice, low–medium delivery.
- Generic delivery apps: medium local choice, high delivery.
- **Flower Man: high local choice, high delivery.**

**11 · Financials (estimates)**
- Revenue: €20k (2027) → €105k → €394k → €858k → €1.54M (2031).
- EBITDA positive in 2031.
- Peak cumulative need ≈ €445k.

**12 · The ask**
**€50k angel round now**, part of a €300k package:
- €50k angels;
- €10k Nuova Impresa grant;
- €240k Smart&Start zero-interest loan.

Investors in an innovative startup deduct **65%** of their investment from IRPEF (max €100k/year, hold 3 years), so €10k invested costs €3.5k after tax.

The money pays for the Bergamo launch, Brescia and forecasting R&D.
